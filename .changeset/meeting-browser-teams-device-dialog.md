Get a Microsoft Teams bot past the device dialog and count its notice posted only once the chat service took it. In the bot's browser, which has no microphone or camera, the Teams client lays "Are you sure you don't want audio or video?" over the pre-join screen, and its backdrop took the Join now click until the join timed out (`join: TimeoutError`). The adapter now presses the dialog's Continue without audio or video key before Join now, and presses Join now in short tries for thirty seconds, so a dialog drawn late is closed first and one gone before its key is pressed leaves the next try to Join now. The client draws the bot's own message at once as "Sending..." and the chat service may refuse it ("Failed to send."), as it refused a guest's first message sent seconds after joining. The adapter counts a message posted only once it shows its time, reads the bot's own messages by the name it joined with so a participant's message with the same words never stands for them, sends a refused message again after five and then ten seconds, names a message refused at all three sends `chat_send_failed`, and never sends again a message the service has not answered within fifteen seconds, naming it `chat_send_unanswered`; either ends the notice step. The bot gives its farewell ten seconds at most before it presses Leave, inside the thirty seconds a bot told to leave has before it is killed. `selectorsVersion` is 2026-10-31.
