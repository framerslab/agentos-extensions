The Google Meet adapter reads the chat panel's composer by its shape and its place while the Chat key says its panel is open, whatever its label, and names a composer the client draws disabled, read-only or aria-disabled `chat_input_blocked` at once, before any text is typed, with the panel's outline and `tried=state:<lookup>`. The nightly real-join check's Meet run of 27 October 2026 (run 40539663181, the bot image at bd30397c) was admitted and left with `notice: chat_input_missing`; its outline read the panel as open (`expanded:true`) with one drawn textarea in the side panel (the complementary landmark), named by an aria-label that begins neither "Send a message" nor "History is o", disabled and aria-disabled: the meeting's chat was off for the bot, a signed-in guest from another organisation, and every lookup keyed on the composer's name, so a composer that was there and would take no text was reported as missing. The new lookup (`chatInputInPanel`: a textarea, a textbox or an editable inside an `aside` or an element with the complementary role) runs after the known shapes. The Chat key's own state (`aria-pressed` or `aria-expanded`: open when either reads true, closed when either reads false; a key that marks neither is trusted once the panel opened on it in the page) says when that lookup may be read, on a post and in the chat observer's reads, so a field of another side panel never stands for the composer while the chat panel is closed, a composer found by the panel lookup alone is read again on every later post (the farewell, a message the manager commands) without a second press of the key, which toggles the panel, and a key that says its panel is open is never pressed. Each lookup reads its first visible match, never its first match, so a hidden match before the composer in document order no longer hides it. The outline's `tried=` names the four lookups (`composer:role,textarea,editable,panel`) when none finds a composer. The lifecycle test's fixture draws the disabled shape as `chat=off` and the enabled composer under another label as `chat=relabelled`, each in a side panel that is an `aside`; the new steps check the report, the outline, that nothing was posted through the disabled composer, and that the notice and then the farewell went through the relabelled one with the panel left open. `selectorsVersion` is 2026-10-27.
