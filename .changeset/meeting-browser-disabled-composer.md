The Google Meet adapter reads the chat panel's composer by its shape and its place once the panel has opened on its key, whatever its label, and names a composer the client draws disabled, read-only or aria-disabled `chat_input_blocked` at once, before any text is typed, with the panel's outline and `tried=state:<lookup>`. The nightly real-join check's Meet run of 27 October 2026 (run 40539663181, the bot image at bd30397c) was admitted and left with `notice: chat_input_missing`; its outline read the panel as open (`expanded:true`) with one drawn textarea in the side panel (the complementary landmark), named by an aria-label that begins neither "Send a message" nor "History is o", disabled and aria-disabled: the meeting's chat was off for the bot, a signed-in guest from another organisation, and every lookup keyed on the composer's name, so a composer that was there and would take no text was reported as missing. The new lookup (`chatInputInPanel`: a textarea, a textbox or an editable inside an `aside` or an element with the complementary role) runs after the known shapes, only once the Chat key has been pressed, and in the chat observer's reads, so a field of another side panel never stands for the composer before the key is pressed. The outline's `tried=` names the four lookups (`composer:role,textarea,editable,panel`) when none finds a composer. The lifecycle test's fixture draws the shape as `chat=off`, in a side panel that is an `aside`, and the test's new step checks the report, the outline and that nothing was posted. `selectorsVersion` is 2026-10-27.
