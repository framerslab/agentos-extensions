The bot image finds the Google Meet chat's composer where a Google Chat conversation's panel draws it: inside a frame the panel embeds, on chat.google.com, with the chat's Send key and its messages. The nightly real-join check's Meet run of 25 October 2026 (run 40152692677, bot image 0.1.3) was admitted, opened the panel on its key and left eleven seconds later with `notice_failed` and `notice: chat_input_missing`: every lookup of 0.1.3 ran through `page.locator`, which Playwright resolves in the page's main frame alone, so a composer drawn in an embedded frame was never looked for. The Meet adapter now looks for the composer, by role and name, as the textarea and as an editable by its shape, in every frame of the page (the main frame first, the frames read anew on each poll, since the chat's frame attaches after the panel opens), types and presses Enter there, presses the Send key in the same frame when Enter left the text, reads its own message back from that frame, and its chat observer reads the panel's messages from the frame the composer is in, so a stop phrase typed in a Google Chat conversation is heard. The step codes and the ten-second bounds stand. The selectors are dated 2026-10-25.

When a step of posting the notice fails, the bot's error report carries, after the step's code, an outline of the chat panel (`image/src/adapters/chat-outline.ts`): for the main frame and each frame the page embeds, the frame's host and its counts of drawn controls by role (textbox, combobox, button, menuitem), of editables, of list items and of elements carrying a message id, the accessible names of the composer-shaped controls (and of the buttons and menu items in an embedded frame, whose controls are the chat's own), each cut to forty characters of letters, digits and a few marks and at most twelve a frame, and the lookups the step tried, for example `notice: chat_input_missing; panel: frames=2; f1 meet.google.com textbox=0 combobox=1 button=23 menuitem=0 editable=0 listitem=0 messageid=0; f2 chat.google.com textbox=1 combobox=0 button=6 menuitem=2 editable=1 listitem=3 messageid=3 names=History is on|Send message; tried=composer:role,textarea,editable`. It never holds a message's text, a participant's name or address, or a link's path or query: a control inside a message element names nothing, a name that quotes a tile's, a sender's or a message's text is dropped, and a frame is named by its host alone. The whole outline is read within three seconds and fits the protocol's ceiling on an error report. The nightly check's record (`real-join.mjs`) keeps such a message whole in reports.json, and its first two hundred characters in the outcome's errors.

The fixture draws two more shapes of the Meet chat: `chat=frame`, the composer, the Send key and the messages inside a frame the page embeds (`/chat-frame`), and `chat=none`, a panel with no composer at all; the lifecycle test posts the notice through the frame's editable, hears the stop phrase typed there, and reads the outline a run on the composerless panel reports, in which neither the message drawn before the panel opened, its sender nor a tile's name appears.
