The bot image finds the Google Meet chat's composer where a Google Chat conversation's panel draws it: inside a frame the panel embeds, on chat.google.com, with the chat's Send key and its messages. The nightly real-join check's Meet run of 25 October 2026 (run 40152692677, bot image 0.1.3) was admitted, opened the panel on its key and left eleven seconds later with `notice_failed` and `notice: chat_input_missing`: every lookup of 0.1.3 ran through `page.locator`, which Playwright resolves in the page's main frame alone, so a composer drawn in an embedded frame was never looked for. The Meet adapter now looks for the composer, by role and name, as the textarea and as an editable by its shape, in every frame of the page (the main frame first, the frames read anew on each poll, since the chat's frame attaches after the panel opens), types and presses Enter there, presses the Send key in the same frame when Enter left the text, reads its own message back from that frame, and its chat observer reads the panel's messages from the frame the composer is in, so a stop phrase typed in a Google Chat conversation is heard. The step codes and the ten-second bounds stand. The selectors are dated 2026-10-25.

When a step of posting the notice fails, the bot's error report carries, after the step's code, an outline of the chat panel (`image/src/adapters/chat-outline.ts`): for the main frame and each frame the page embeds, the frame's host and its counts of drawn controls by role (textbox, combobox, button, menuitem), of editables, of list items and of elements carrying a message id, the accessible names of the composer-shaped controls (and of the buttons in an embedded frame, whose controls are the chat's own; a menu item is counted and never named, since no lookup targets one and a menu may list people), each cut to forty characters of letters, digits and a few marks and at most twelve a frame after the filter below, and the lookups the step tried, for example `notice: chat_input_missing; panel: frames=2; f1 meet.google.com textbox=0 combobox=1 button=23 menuitem=0 editable=0 listitem=0 messageid=0; f2 chat.google.com textbox=1 combobox=0 button=6 menuitem=2 editable=1 listitem=3 messageid=3 names=History is on|Send message; tried=composer:role,textarea,editable`. It never holds an address or a link, nor a message's text or a participant's name that the frames draw: a control inside a message element names nothing; a name is tested whole, before any cut, and is dropped when it holds the whole of a tile's, a sender's or a message's text (a message's text read from the element marked as its text, from the element carrying its id, which is all a Google Chat conversation marks, from an item of a list, and from each element of the message that holds no other, so its sender's name and each of its lines count on their own), when such a text holds the whole of it, when it shares a run of three words with one, or when it holds a word of a participant's name, however the name spells them, each text compared whole, read both as the page gives it and with each element in it a word apart (a sender's name beside a message's first line, two lines split by a line break, a tile's name beside an icon's word), with a mark between two words read both as nothing and as a space (`alpha/beta`), and a text or a word of fewer than three characters matched only as a word of the name, except in a script whose words run together (Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Thai, Lao, Khmer, Burmese), where it is matched anywhere in the name and each letter also counts as a word of a run; a name longer than two hundred characters is dropped without its comparison; a name that holds anything shaped like an address or a link (an `@` address, a scheme such as `https://`, `mailto:` or `tel:`, a protocol-relative `//`, a bare host such as `docs.example.com` with or without a path after it, or an IPv4 or an IPv6 address), read as written, as a browser reads a host (full-width forms folded, a zero-width space or a soft hyphen dropped, ideographic full stops read as dots) or as it would be shown, is dropped whole, never cleaned and kept; the names are withheld altogether (`names=withheld`) when any frame went unread or any frame's reading stopped at its element limit, since the texts they must not quote may then be unread, and when those texts come to more than twenty thousand terms, which bounds the filter's work; and a frame is named by its host alone. A text the frames do not draw as a message, a sender or a tile (a message outside the part of a long conversation the panel shows, a participant with no tile) is unknown to the filter. The frames are read at once, all of them within three seconds, a frame that does not answer in time written as `unread`, and the whole fits the protocol's ceiling on an error report. The nightly check's record (`real-join.mjs`) keeps such a message whole in reports.json, and its first two hundred characters in the outcome's errors.

The fixture draws two more shapes of the Meet chat: `chat=frame`, the composer, the Send key and the messages inside a frame the page embeds (`/chat-frame`), and `chat=none`, a panel with no composer at all; the lifecycle test posts the notice through the frame's editable, hears the stop phrase typed there, and reads the outline a run on the composerless panel reports, in which neither the message drawn before the panel opened, its sender nor a tile's name appears.
