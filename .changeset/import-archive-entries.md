Add bounded discovery of a ZIP archive's entries for the import walker, under `src/import/archive.ts`, over the `./archive` reader. `discoverArchive` opens the archive under the caller's entry-count and directory-size bounds and lists every entry from its central directory without inflating any, counting each file at its recorded size, and at the depth its path spells, against the caller's `ImportLimits`, skipped files included, and listing each folder once, whether an entry's path implies it or the archive lists it empty. A file that is too large, a link, an unsafe path, an archive by its name or compressed other than stored or DEFLATE is listed as skipped and never gets a reader. A complete manifest comes with one reader per ready file, which reads that entry alone, inflated in the reader's worker, under the per-file bound, one entry of the archive at a time, and yields it as one chunk, the archive free for the next read once that chunk is handed on; `close()` or the discovery's signal ends every read as `stopped`. Discovery cut short by a bound, a repeated path, a file where a folder is, an unsafe folder name, a corrupt archive or the signal says why with a fixed reason and comes with no readers. `projectArchiveSelection` places an archive's rows under one named folder, as a chosen root, and keeps the archive's own bytes apart from the bytes its entries inflate to. No package entry exports it yet; the existing entries are unchanged.
