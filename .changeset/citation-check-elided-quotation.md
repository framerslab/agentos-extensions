A quotation with gaps (an ellipsis or a bracketed insertion) is `exact` only when all its pieces stand in one block of the page: a paragraph, a list item, a table cell, a caption or a quotation. A sentence of one paragraph and a table cell further down the page no longer pass as one quotation. When an exact quotation's pieces run past one sentence, its passage is their block.
