Tune a connection's operating bounds without a reset. `control(scope, { type: 'policy_tuned' }, policy)` stores a policy that differs from the stored one only in the nine operating bounds `SOURCE_SYNC_OPERATING_BOUNDS` names (`scanPageSize`, `maxScanPages`, `maxCandidates`, `maxChangedPerLease`, `maxHistoryPagesPerLease`, `leaseMs`, `pollMs`, `manualMinIntervalMs` and `reconcileMs`), under the owner fence's row lock and the connection's, with no barrier and neither a new generation nor a new owner epoch: the cursor, `revalidationRequired`, progress, schedule, open cycle, retained sources, leases, fence tickets and derived results stay, so search and delivery go on. The new bounds apply from the next lease. While a lease is live the tune changes nothing and answers null, so a sync never sees its policy change under it, and a scan left open between leases that has already reached a lower `maxScanPages` or `maxCandidates` ends there, as a bounded scan ends. A tune whose policy differs in any other key, the window and the retained, held and indexed ceilings among them and any key a host adds, throws the new `SourceSyncPolicyTuneError`, whose `bounds` names those keys as the new `sourceSyncContentChanges(stored, next)` answers them; such a change still takes `policy_changed` and its reset. The state machine's `policy_tuned` event moves only `revision` and `updatedAt`, in any state.
