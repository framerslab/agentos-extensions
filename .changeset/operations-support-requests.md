Support requests are built: `operations.tickets` holds the person's side (`open`, `list`, `get`, `read`, `write`, `share`, `resolve`, `remove`, `upload`, `picture`, `stream`), staff's side (`staffList` with its counts and 7-day line, `staffGet`, `reply`, `claim`, `setPriority`, `setState`, `openIdentity`, `writeTo`, `staffRemove`, `stats`), `systemMessage` for the product's own events, `hangup` for a product's account deletion step, and `sendDue` and `notifyDue`, which drain the mail and bell outboxes. Each request carries an anonymous id of its own, drawn again on a collision up to five times; the quota and cooldown come from one COUNT; a live stream begins with the request's state and carries each message and state change over `listen` and `notify` on PostgreSQL and a timed read on SQLite; pictures are decoded with sharp and written again as WebP with no metadata; a link to a token path, a `www.` address with no scheme included, is cut before it is stored; the person's list pages from the last request shown; `deleteAccount` and `exportAccount` are built, the export a zip stream with `requests.json` and one file a picture, three a day. The hourly sweep resolves a request after seven days of the person's silence, closes one 30 days after it was resolved and removes uploads no message named within an hour. `./react` holds the headless hooks (the stream hook waits twice as long after each failed connection and stops after a refusal the server will keep giving) and the sanitized markdown renderer (http, https and mailto links alone). An actor is a person by its `plan` word or a staff member by its `role` word, never both. The support tables shipped with 0.1.0; no migration is added. `errors` and `health` still throw `not_built` until the next minor release.
